
## This reel has an on-camera presenter (AI character): write it like a premium creator reel
The presenter speaks the hook (the first ~5-7 seconds) and the call to action (the last ~5-7 seconds) to camera; clips, our
3D shots and graphics carry the middle. Style: {{style}}.

Psychology that keeps people watching and makes them act, honestly:
- Hook (first 1.5 s): a pattern interrupt aimed at one person: a sharp question, a surprising exact number, or a common
  belief turned over ("Most students waste their first lakh on the wrong course"). Name the viewer (class 12 students,
  parents) so they feel spoken to.
- Open a loop in the first 3 seconds and close it only near the end ("the third cost is the one nobody tells you").
- Specific beats vague: exact hours, rules and amounts from the verified facts only, never rounded up to sound bigger.
- Loss framing is allowed when it is true (what a wrong choice really costs); social proof only from real, verified facts.
- One clear next step at the end (comment the keyword / save it / send it to a parent), said by the presenter.
- Never: fake urgency or scarcity ("only 2 seats left"), invented testimonials, guaranteed jobs or salaries, fear that is
  not supported by the facts. These break trust and DGCA advertising rules, and Instagram demotes them.
Short spoken lines (4-9 words), contractions, natural pauses; the presenter sounds like a friendly senior pilot, not an ad.

Graphics for this style:
- studio: calm 2-4 s shots in the middle; use `behind_text` for the 2-3 key words of the reel and `ui_card` for a rule, a
  fee line or a question; keep `big_text`, `list_card`, `counter` and `compare` for the middle (they are hidden while the
  presenter is on screen, so they never cover the face).
- fast: a new shot every 1.2-2 s in the middle (the picture also punches in every ~0.7 s automatically); one `behind_text`
  per group, short `chip`s, `flash`/`shake` on reveals; still never more than two graphics on screen.
