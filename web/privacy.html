Privacy Policy

Reel Studio, operated by We One Aviation, New Delhi, India. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

What Reel Studio is

Reel Studio is an internal tool used by the We One Aviation team to produce short educational aviation videos and publish them to We One Aviation's own Instagram account, Facebook Page and LinkedIn Company Page. It is not offered to the public, and only team members invited by the owner can sign in.

Data we use

  • Team sign-in: the email address and password of invited team members (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Social accounts: access tokens for We One Aviation's own Instagram professional account, Facebook Page and LinkedIn Company Page, granted by an administrator of those accounts. They are used only to publish our videos and to read the basic account details needed to do so (account name, page identifier, post links).
  • Content: the videos, captions and fact sources we create.

We do not collect, read or store data about other LinkedIn or Instagram members, followers or commenters, and we do not use any data for advertising, profiling or sale.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a Supabase project (database and file storage). Access tokens are kept server side, are never shown in the website, and can be removed at any time with Disconnect, or by revoking the app's access in Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn settings. Videos in cloud storage are deleted after 30 days.

Sharing

Content is shared only by publishing it, at our request, to our own Instagram account, Facebook Page and LinkedIn Company Page through their official APIs. No other third parties receive the data.
